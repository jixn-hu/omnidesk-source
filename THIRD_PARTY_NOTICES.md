Dependency versions are recorded in package-lock.json and native build manifests. Original notices for major remote-access components and the Windows runtime are included in [docs/licenses](docs/licenses/).

Electron, Chromium, Node.js, React and other dependencies retain their own copyright and license files. The packaged Electron runtime provides its own LICENSE and LICENSES.chromium.html; do not remove these files from redistributed packages. Source and development dependencies can be inspected through the lockfile and installed packages.

Windows runtime binaries are generated by tools/build-freerdp.cjs from the pinned FreeRDP revision and the developer's MSYS2 environment; the build collects actual dependency DLLs and their notices. There are no prebuilt native binaries in this source repository. When rebuilding or redistributing, review the licenses of the exact toolchain and dependency versions used, and preserve the collected notices.

Motion uses its MIT license, retained in docs/licenses/Motion-LICENSE. The monitoring agent uses Go, gopsutil and their dependencies under their respective licenses; tools/build-monitor-agent.cjs collects the exact dependency notices and THIRD_PARTY.json into the distribution.
